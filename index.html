Have Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about rooftop solar in Tamil Nadu, TNEB net-metering, and government subsidies.

How much government subsidy can I get for rooftop solar?

Under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, eligible residential consumers can receive up to ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, and up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW and higher systems. The subsidy is deposited directly into your bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) after successful DISCOM commissioning.

Can solar completely eliminate my EB bill?

Solar can cut your bi-monthly bill by up to 90%. Fixed or minimum utility charges may still apply, depending on your tariff.

What solar system size do I need for my home?

It depends on your connected load, average consumption and shade-free terrace area. Our engineer recommends the right size after the site assessment.

What is PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana?

It is a national scheme that provides Central Financial Assistance to eligible residential consumers who install rooftop solar.

Do you help with the subsidy paperwork and DISCOM approvals?

Yes. We handle documentation, national portal uploads, DISCOM inspections and net-meter commissioning end to end.

What is bi-directional net metering?

A bi-directional meter records the power you import from the grid and the surplus solar power you export, so you are billed only on the net usage.

How much rooftop space is required?

As a rough guide, about 10 sq m of shadow-free terrace per kW. Our engineers confirm this during the site visit.

Does a rooftop solar system require regular maintenance?

Very little. Periodic panel cleaning and an annual inspection are usually enough.

What happens during cloudy days or monsoon seasons?

Generation dips on overcast days, and the grid supplies the balance automatically through net metering.

Can I install solar if I have limited rooftop space?

Often yes. Elevated structures and optimised layouts can help, and we assess feasibility during the free site visit.

How long does a rooftop solar system last?

Quality solar panels typically last 25+ years. Inverters may need replacement earlier.
